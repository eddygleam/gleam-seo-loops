04 · FLAGS

Problems ranking data hides

Cannibalisation — two Gleam URLs competing

KeywordCPCURL AURL BFlips (4wk)KeepRecommended action
How to de-cannibalise — in this order. Skipping step 4 is why cannibalisation comes back.
  1. Pick the winner on evidence, not preference. Count ranking keywords in the cluster, referring domains, and conversion rate per session. Highest of the three wins. Never pick on which page you prefer or which is newer.
  2. Choose redirect or differentiate. If the losing page has no independent job → 301 to the winner. If it genuinely serves different intent (a template someone lands on from inside the app) → keep it and retarget it. Canonical tags are the wrong tool here: Google treats them as a hint and the flipping often continues.
  3. Strip the competing signal. On the losing page, remove the target keyword from title, H1 and first paragraph. Give it a modifier the winner is not chasing.
  4. Repoint every internal link. Any internal anchor using the target keyword must point at the winner. This is the step that actually resolves it — the rest is housekeeping.
  5. Update the sitemap and remove the redirected URL.
  6. Re-submit the winner via gsc:inspect_url_enhanced so the change is picked up in days rather than weeks.
  7. Watch four weeks. One URL should hold consistently. Continued flipping means the two pages are still too similar — go back to step 3.
